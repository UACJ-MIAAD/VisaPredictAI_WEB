Cleaning and feature engineering (FE)

An automatic census of the pipeline’s master decisions —12 cleaning and 8 feature-construction decisions—, each documented with its real module and its why. On the cleaning side: the C/F/U/UNK administrative regime is preserved as annotation and only cells with a specific date (status F) are the predictive target (F-only mask); a UNK sentinel instead of the literal NA; a century pivot with an epoch guard (t0 = 1975-01-01); a parser tolerant to footnotes and typos that never corrects silently (the raw cell is always kept in raw_value); deduplication preferring F>C>U>UNK; gaps of up to 3 months interpolated linearly and long ones never invented (Kalman smoothing for the EDA only); retrogressions and extreme jumps are signal —they are counted, never trimmed—; and the contract is re-verified with declarative CHECKs in the star-schema warehouse.

On the feature side: the target is days since the fixed epoch (status F only); a regular monthly grid; the trees predict the first difference and the forecast is reintegrated causally, because a tree cannot extrapolate the level; the fiscal calendar is encoded cyclically with sine/cosine (it starts in October: December and January are neighbors); 24 monthly lags as memory; scaling fitted only on the training window (no leakage from the future into the past); an explicit covariate policy per model family; and a FRESH selection (with Benjamini-Yekutieli FDR correction) plus mRMR de-redundancy that collapses the characterization catalog down to a single non-redundant representative (44 → 1 in the current cut). An annotated gallery of seven figures illustrates the decisions —from the tolerant parser and the F-only mask to differencing and the cyclic calendar.
