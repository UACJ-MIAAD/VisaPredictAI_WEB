Exploratory data analysis (EDA)

An automatic, series-by-series census of the full panel: 27,611 observations across 194 structural series (136 with at least one specific date and 74 evaluable), over 296/296 bulletins since December 2001. 58 % of the panel is trainable (15,931 trainable obs, status F); the census also documents 382 historical retrogressions and the 1,647 Diversity Visa (DV) lottery rows. The ADF, KPSS and DF-GLS stationarity tests indicate that 71 of the 74 evaluable series require differencing. An annotated gallery of eleven figures accompanies the census —from the full panel’s regime map to the family trajectories, the FAD–DFF gap and the fiscal-year pulse. The full report is a downloadable PDF, regenerated automatically with every new bulletin.
